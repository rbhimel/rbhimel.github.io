National University Gazipur servers host records for over 2 million students nationwide. When exam routines or semester results publish, hundreds of thousands of concurrent requests overwhelm their infrastructure. Our scraper monitors the servers continuously in the background and delivers notice details and PDF archives directly to Telegram so you never have to wait for pages to load.
